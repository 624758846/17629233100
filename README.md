# DTU Collection Project

## How to Cancel Auto-Renewal

If you have an active subscription with auto-renewal enabled, you can cancel it at any time. Follow these steps to cancel your auto-renewal:

### Method 1: Through the Application

1. Open the DTU Collection application
2. Navigate to **Settings** or **Account Settings**
3. Look for **Subscription** or **Billing** section
4. Find the **Auto-Renewal** option
5. Click **Cancel Auto-Renewal** or toggle the auto-renewal switch to **OFF**
6. Confirm your cancellation when prompted

### Method 2: Through Your Account Portal

1. Visit the account portal at your service provider's website
2. Log in with your credentials
3. Go to **My Account** > **Subscriptions**
4. Find the DTU Collection subscription
5. Click **Manage Subscription**
6. Select **Cancel Auto-Renewal**
7. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the cancellation

### Method 3: Contact Support

If you're unable to cancel through the above methods, you can contact customer support:

- **Email**: Contact your service provider's support email
- **Phone**: Contact your service provider's support phone line
- **Live Chat**: May be available through the application or service provider's website

> **Note**: Replace the above with your actual service provider's contact information.

### Important Information

- **Cancellation Timing**: You can cancel auto-renewal at any time before the next billing cycle
- **Access Period**: After canceling auto-renewal, you will retain access to the service until the end of your current billing period
- **Refunds**: Canceling auto-renewal does not automatically trigger a refund for the current period. For refund inquiries, please contact support
- **Reactivation**: You can reactivate auto-renewal at any time through the same settings menu

### Confirmation

After successfully canceling auto-renewal, you should receive:
- An on-screen confirmation message
- A confirmation email to your registered email address

If you do not receive confirmation within 24 hours, please contact customer support to verify the cancellation.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Will I lose access immediately after canceling auto-renewal?**  
A: No, you will continue to have access until the end of your current billing period.

**Q: Can I get a refund after canceling?**  
A: Refund policies vary. Please contact customer support for specific refund requests.

**Q: How do I know if auto-renewal is canceled?**  
A: Check your account settings. The auto-renewal option should show as "OFF" or "Disabled", and you should receive a confirmation email.

**Q: Can I reactivate auto-renewal later?**  
A: Yes, you can turn auto-renewal back on at any time through your account settings.

## Project Information

This is a WPF application project for DTU (Data Terminal Unit) collection and management.

### Requirements

- .NET Framework 4.x or higher
- Windows operating system

### Building the Project

1. Open `DTU_Collection_Prj.sln` in Visual Studio
2. Restore NuGet packages
3. Build the solution (Ctrl+Shift+B)

### Running the Application

After building, run the application from Visual Studio or execute the compiled .exe file from the bin directory.
